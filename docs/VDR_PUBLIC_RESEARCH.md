## 1. Executive Summary & Research Scope

A comprehensive digital intelligence audit of publicly available listings, local business directories, trade registries, and regional service maps for **Vijaya Durga Refrigeration** (Ravulapalem, Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Konaseema District, Andhra Pradesh) was conducted.

The investigation revealed that Vijaya Durga Refrigeration operates a dual-model enterprise:
1. **Showroom Sales & Display**: Retail sales of branded residential air conditioners, washing machines, and appliances.
2. **Technical Workshop & Spares Counter**: Field service, installation, preventative maintenance, gas charging, and genuine replacement spare parts distribution for HVAC and home refrigeration across the Godavari delta region.

1. **Current Physical Floor Inventory**:
   - Exact quantities and models currently in the Ravulapalem showroom.
   - Whether floor models are 2025/2026 BEE energy star label compliant.
2. **Authorized Dealership Agreements**:
   - Are Daikin, Lloyd, and Mitsubishi Electric direct first-party distributorships or master dealer tie-ups?
   - Is there an active retail dealership for Godrej, Whirlpool, or LG, or are they serviced on an ad-hoc basis?
3. **Consumer Pricing & Quotation Policy**:
   - Official policy on whether prices should be published online or hidden behind "Contact Showroom for Best Walk-in Price" (standard practice among regional dealers to protect margins).
4. **Official Helplines**:
   - Direct personal mobile number for WhatsApp customer enquiries vs showroom landline.
   - Designated technician dispatch contact for emergency service calls.
5. **Operating Schedule**:
   - Exact opening and closing hours on weekdays, Saturdays, and Sundays/holidays.
